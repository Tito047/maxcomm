Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom 3D printed busts, policies, and process.

What kind of photos do I need to provide?
To create an accurate 3D model, we require at least 4 clear, well-lit photos of the subject's face: one directly from the front, one from the left profile, one from the right profile, and one from the back. Ensure the subject's hair is visible and not heavily obscured.
If the subject is not available. We need videos and pictures covering the subject roughly. Our animators will use sketching tools and AI tools to reconstruct the 3D model of the subject.
What materials and printers do you use?
We exclusively utilize the Bambu Lab A1 3D printing system alongside premium PLA filaments. This setup allows us to achieve incredibly smooth surface finishes, high-fidelity vertex rendering, and micrometer dimensional accuracy that standard printers simply cannot match.
Will I get to see the design before it is printed?
Absolutely. Before we start the physical manufacturing process, we will send you a digital 3D preview of the sculpted model. We only proceed to the printing stage once you have explicitly approved the design.
How do shipping and COD fees work?
Prepaid orders valued over Rs 1,000 enjoy entirely free shipping. Orders under Rs 1,000 carry a standard Rs 150 shipping fee. If you choose Cash on Delivery (COD), an additional Rs 150 convenience handling fee is applied by our delivery partners.
What is your cancellation and return policy?
If you choose not to approve the 3D digital preview, we will cancel your order and issue a full refund immediately. However, because each bust is a personalized, custom-manufactured item, no cancellations, modifications, or returns are accepted once the 3D preview is approved and production begins.
